Iranian officials denounced the U.S. sanctions announced this week, calling them provocative measures that undermine dialogue and sovereignty. State media outlets including Tehran Times and Press TV published statements from government spokespeople criticizing the penalties.

An Iranian foreign ministry spokesperson said the sanctions target economic activity and would prompt a considered response. "We will take proportionate measures to defend our national interests," the spokesperson said in comments carried by state outlets.
