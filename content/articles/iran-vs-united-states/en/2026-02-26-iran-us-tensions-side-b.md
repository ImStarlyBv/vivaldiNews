Iran's official channels condemned the United States' announcement of new sanctions, describing the measures as further escalation and a barrier to constructive dialogue. Iranian spokespeople insisted the country reserves the right to respond to what it described as economic aggression.

State-affiliated analysts suggested that Tehran may pursue reciprocal economic measures or increase diplomatic pressure in international forums. Observers caution that both sides still have openings for negotiation if mediated talks resume.
