Poland scrambled fighter jets and temporarily shut down two airports in its eastern regions on Thursday after Russian drones were detected approaching Polish airspace during a massive aerial bombardment of western Ukraine — an escalation that once again brought the war dangerously close to NATO territory.

The Polish military confirmed that F-16 fighters were deployed as part of a coordinated air defense response, while Rzeszów-Jasionka and Lublin airports were briefly closed to civilian traffic. Polish airspace monitoring systems tracked several Russian drone trajectories that came within kilometers of the border.

## Ukraine Under Siege

The barrage — which Kyiv described as one of the largest since the start of 2026 — targeted energy infrastructure, military installations, and civilian areas across western and central Ukraine. The Ukrainian Air Force reported intercepting over 80 of approximately 120 missiles and drones launched overnight, but several struck critical power facilities in Lviv and Ivano-Frankivsk oblasts, leaving tens of thousands without electricity.

President Volodymyr Zelensky called the attack "another act of terror against our people" and urged Western allies to accelerate deliveries of advanced air defense systems. "Every day without adequate air defense costs Ukrainian lives," Zelensky wrote on social media.

## NATO on Alert

The incident prompted an emergency consultation among NATO allies. Poland's Prime Minister confirmed that Article 4 consultations — in which a member state raises a threat to its security — were being considered. "Poland will not tolerate threats to its airspace or its citizens," he stated.

The Polish response underscored the persistent risk of the war spilling beyond Ukraine's borders. In December 2022, a stray missile killed two people in the Polish village of Przewodów, and since then, NATO's eastern flank has maintained heightened readiness.

Ukraine's Foreign Minister called on the international community to establish a no-fly zone over western Ukraine, arguing that the proximity of Russian strikes to NATO territory made the case "undeniable." Western officials have so far resisted such calls, fearing direct confrontation with Russia.

## The Human Cost

With an estimated 500,000 killed on both sides since February 2022 — according to a recent Le Monde investigation — the war has become Europe's deadliest conflict since World War II. Ukrainian officials say the continued targeting of civilian infrastructure is a deliberate strategy to break the population's will, a claim supported by multiple international human rights organizations.
