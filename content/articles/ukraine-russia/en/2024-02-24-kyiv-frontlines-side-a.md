Ukrainian military officials confirmed on Sunday that defensive positions east of Kharkiv remain intact despite increased Russian shelling over the past 48 hours.

## Situation on the Ground

The Ukrainian General Staff reported that forces successfully repelled multiple infantry assaults in the Kharkiv region.

"Our troops are holding firm," said a spokesperson for the Ukrainian armed forces.

## International Support

Western allies continued to express solidarity with Ukraine, reaffirming commitments to long-term military assistance packages.
